# AI Change Handoff

Use this format at task completion. Remove headings that do not apply, but keep it self-contained and factual.

```markdown
## Changed
- `path`: outcome and reason.

## Verification
- `command` — passed/failed/not run (reason).

## Data/security impact
- Rules, fields, roles, cache, logs, integrations or migrations affected; or “none”.

## Remaining work or decisions
- Exact owner decision or limitation and dependent action; or “none”.

## Git/deployment
- Branch/commit/push/deploy status. Do not imply authorization or an action that did not occur.
```
